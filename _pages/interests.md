## Chess

Chess is one of my interests outside research. These are the portals I use or recommend for playing, studying, and following the game:

- [Lichess](https://lichess.org/) for free online games, studies, puzzles, and analysis.
- [Chess.com](https://www.chess.com/) for online play, lessons, puzzles, and events.
- [FIDE](https://www.fide.com/) for the international federation, ratings, titles, and official news.
- [ChessTempo](https://chesstempo.com/) for tactical training and calculation practice.
- [365Chess](https://www.365chess.com/) for searching master games and opening statistics.

This page can grow over time with favourite openings, tournament notes, games, and other interests.
